In `pyproject.toml`, find the `[project.optional-dependencies]` section and update the `gpu` group.

**Current:**
```toml
gpu = [
    "silero-vad>=6.1",
    "librosa>=0.10",
    "opencv-python-headless>=4.9",
]
```

**Change to:**
```toml
gpu = [
    "silero-vad>=6.2,<7",
    "librosa>=0.11,<1",
    "opencv-python-headless>=4.13,<5",
]
```

Rationale (from 08-RESEARCH.md "State of the Art" table):
- `silero-vad>=6.2,<7`: v6.2.0 has edge-case improvements over 6.1; upper bound prevents surprise major version breaks
- `librosa>=0.11,<1`: v0.11 has the keyword-only `y=` parameter in `rms()` that noise_match.py uses; upper bound prevents surprise breaks
- `opencv-python-headless>=4.13,<5`: v4.13 is current stable, requires numpy>=2 on py>=3.9; upper bound prevents surprise OpenCV 5 breaks

Do NOT change any other section of pyproject.toml. The torch/torchaudio uv.sources routing to pytorch-cu128 is already correct. Do NOT change the `enhancement` group or `dev` group.
  </action>
