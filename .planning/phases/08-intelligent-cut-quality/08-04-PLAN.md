**Step C — Wire into render.py:**

Add two new passes in the render pipeline, AFTER word-boundary snapping but BEFORE filtergraph assembly:

1. **De-breathing pass**: If `smoothing.de_breathing_enabled`, iterate over cut boundaries. For each cut-out point, call `detect_breath_extension()` and extend the trim if a breath is detected. Log the extension amount with structlog.

2. **Noise-floor matching pass**: If `smoothing.noise_floor_match_enabled`, for each join between kept segments, call `measure_rms_db()` on the tail of the left segment and head of the right segment.
